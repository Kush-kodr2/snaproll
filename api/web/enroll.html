What happens to my photos? Please read first
  • Purpose: marking your attendance in SnapRoll. Nothing else.
  • What is stored: a list of numbers (a "face template") computed from your photos. The photos themselves are not saved.
  • Who can access it: only the project owner, Kush. Teachers see attendance results, not face templates.
  • How long: until #date, or until you withdraw, whichever comes first.
  • Withdrawing: you can delete your face data at any time with the withdraw code you receive after enrolling, at /withdraw, or by contacting [CONTACT].
  • Enrolling is voluntary. If you don't enroll, your attendance is taken the usual way.
